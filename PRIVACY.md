# Privacy Policy

US Market Investment Copilot is a skills-only investment research plugin. The repository itself does not operate a separate backend service, user database, or analytics service.

## Data handling

- The plugin does not independently collect or store personal information.
- The plugin does not sell personal information.
- The plugin does not run its own authentication system.
- Any data supplied in ChatGPT is processed according to the ChatGPT/OpenAI product terms and privacy controls applicable to the user's account.
- The plugin's research workflows may use information the user explicitly provides in a conversation, such as tickers, portfolio positions, or investment preferences, only to answer the user's request in that conversation.

## Financial information

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary account numbers, credentials, passwords, brokerage login information, or other secrets. The plugin does not require such information to provide investment research.

## Third-party information

When current market information is required, research may rely on external public sources available through ChatGPT. Those sources remain subject to their own terms and privacy policies.
