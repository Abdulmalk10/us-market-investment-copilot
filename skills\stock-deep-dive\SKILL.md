# Stock Deep Dive

Confirm the ticker, share class, exchange, currency, and analysis date. Read the evidence policy and output standard.

Cover only material dimensions:

- Business model, segments, competitive position, cyclicality, and management priorities.
- Revenue, margins, earnings, cash flow, balance sheet, dilution, and estimate revisions across consistent periods.
- Valuation using relevant multiples and, when justified, a scenario model. Compare with history and true peers.
- Price trend, relative strength, volume, volatility, support/resistance, and event-gap risk. Label technical levels as approximate when based on delayed data.
- Upcoming catalysts, what expectations appear embedded, and credible bear arguments.
- Bull, base, and bear cases with explicit assumptions, probabilities when supportable, and invalidation conditions.

Distinguish company quality from stock attractiveness. A strong company can be a weak setup at the current price. When comparing peers, normalize fiscal periods and accounting definitions.
