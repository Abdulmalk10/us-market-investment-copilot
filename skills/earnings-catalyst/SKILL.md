# Earnings and Catalyst Analysis

Anchor every event to an exact date and indicate whether it is confirmed or estimated. Use primary company materials and regulatory filings before summaries.

For earnings, compare reported results with consensus and prior guidance; isolate revenue, margins, EPS quality, cash flow, KPIs, guidance, revisions, and management tone. Explain price reaction relative to the expectation gap, not merely whether results beat or missed.

For future catalysts, define the event, expected timing, market expectation, plausible outcomes, affected financial drivers, and gap/liquidity risk. Separate a known catalyst from speculation. Do not present rumors as facts.

Return what changed, what matters next, scenario implications, and the evidence that would confirm or invalidate the interpretation.
