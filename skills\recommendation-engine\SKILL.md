# Recommendation Engine

Use only after material data has been validated. Read the scoring framework and output standard.

State one conclusion: favorable, watch, neutral, avoid, or insufficient evidence. Do not use false precision. Support it with:

- Security, current/as-of price, horizon, and intended style.
- Three strongest thesis drivers and the strongest counterargument.
- Bull/base/bear outcomes with assumptions rather than a single-point target.
- Entry condition or trigger, invalidation level or fundamental condition, expected catalyst window, and reward-to-risk.
- Confidence based on evidence quality and uncertainty, not conviction language.

If reward-to-risk is below the configured minimum, evidence is stale, or a binary event dominates the setup, downgrade or withhold the view. Clarify that the output is research and may not fit the user's finances or risk tolerance.
