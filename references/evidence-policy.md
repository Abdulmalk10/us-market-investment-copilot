# Evidence policy

Use current sources for prices, news, estimates, filings, calendars, management, regulations, and market conditions. Cite claims near the statement they support.

Source priority:

1. SEC filings, issuer investor-relations materials, exchanges, regulators, central banks, and official statistics.
2. Established market-data and consensus providers.
3. Reputable financial reporting for context.
4. Social posts and forums only as sentiment or idea-generation inputs, never sole evidence for a material fact.

Record the publication date, effective period, and as-of time where relevant. Use filing dates rather than assuming a fiscal period was publicly known earlier. Label calculations, estimates, interpretations, and rumors. When sources conflict, compare definitions and timestamps instead of selecting the preferred figure.
