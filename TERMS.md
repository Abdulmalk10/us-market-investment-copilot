# Terms of Use

US Market Investment Copilot provides investment research, market analysis, and decision-support workflows. It is not a brokerage service, fiduciary service, portfolio management service, or guarantee of investment performance.

## Investment risk

- Market investments can lose value, including the full amount invested.
- Research outputs may contain uncertainty, estimates, assumptions, delayed information, or incomplete data.
- Users remain responsible for their own investment decisions and for verifying material information before acting.
- No output should be interpreted as a guaranteed return or risk-free recommendation.

## Intended use

The plugin is intended to help users organize and evaluate public-market information, compare securities, understand catalysts and risks, and construct bull/base/bear scenarios.

## Data and sources

The plugin may rely on public market information and sources available through ChatGPT. Source availability, timing, and accuracy can vary. Material numerical or time-sensitive claims should be checked against authoritative sources when possible.

## No transaction execution

This skills-only plugin does not itself execute securities trades, access brokerage credentials, transfer funds, or custody assets.

These terms may be updated as the plugin evolves.
